Mapping meals around cruciferous vegetables, fermented foods, protein timing, and hydration goals is a lot to hold in mind on top of an already full life. If the complete approach feels like too much right now, here's the version that captures the core benefit with minimal planning.

## The one grocery addition worth making

Add one cruciferous vegetable, broccoli, cauliflower, or brussels sprouts, to your cart this week and work it into two or three meals however's easiest, roasted as a side, tossed into a stir-fry, blended into a soup. You don't need a specific recipe, just the ingredient showing up regularly.

## The one habit worth adding daily

A small serving of a fermented food, a spoonful of sauerkraut, a cup of plain yogurt, added to something you're already eating covers the gut-support piece without requiring a new recipe or meal built around it specifically.

## What to do about protein without overthinking it

Make sure something with protein shows up at each meal, eggs, chicken, beans, yogurt, without needing to calculate exact grams. This single habit covers most of the benefit of more precise protein timing without any tracking involved.

## What to skip for now

Detailed meal plans, tracking hydration ounces, and precise timing around your training schedule can all wait. The grocery addition and daily fermented food habit cover the highest-impact pieces without asking for a system to maintain.

## What if you don't like the typical estrogen-supportive foods

Cruciferous vegetables and fermented foods have plenty of variety within each category, if broccoli isn't appealing, brussels sprouts or cabbage cover the same DIM-related benefit, and if sauerkraut isn't your thing, plain yogurt or kimchi work just as well for the fermented food piece. Finding one version of each category you genuinely enjoy matters more than forcing down a specific food you dislike.

## A simple way to remember this window

Since this phase starts right after your period, a marker you're likely already aware of, tying these two small habits to that same awareness removes the need for a separate tracking system.

## What this simplified version can and can't do

This captures a meaningful piece of the benefit without the full detail of a mapped-out plan. If energy or other symptoms during this window don't improve after a couple of cycles, building out more structure, more variety, more precise timing, makes sense once you have more bandwidth for it.
