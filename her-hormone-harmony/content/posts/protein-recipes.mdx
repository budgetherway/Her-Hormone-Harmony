Hitting a higher protein target during a deficit gets harder when every high-protein recipe you know feels the same after a few weeks. These five spread protein across breakfast, lunch, dinner, and a snack, all built to satisfy without needing to eat the same three meals on repeat.

## Egg White and Veggie Breakfast Muffins

A make-ahead breakfast that delivers meaningful protein without much morning effort, since these bake in a batch and reheat well through the week.

**Ingredients:** 8 egg whites, 2 whole eggs, 1 cup diced bell pepper, 1/2 cup spinach, 1/4 cup diced onion, salt, pepper.

**Instructions:** Whisk eggs and egg whites, stir in vegetables. Pour into a greased muffin tin, bake at 350°F for 20 minutes until set.

## Grilled Chicken and Quinoa Power Bowl

A straightforward, high-protein lunch built around lean chicken and a complete-protein grain, easy to meal prep in bulk for several days at once.

**Ingredients:** 2 chicken breasts, 1 cup cooked quinoa, 2 cups mixed roasted vegetables, 2 tbsp olive oil, lemon juice, herbs.

**Instructions:** Season and grill chicken until cooked through, slice. Serve over quinoa with roasted vegetables, drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice.

## Baked Cod with White Beans and Greens

A lighter dinner that still hits a real protein number, pairing lean fish with beans for an additional protein and fiber boost in the same meal.

**Ingredients:** 2 cod fillets, 1 can white beans, 3 cups kale, 2 tbsp olive oil, garlic, lemon.

**Instructions:** Bake cod at 400°F for 12 to 15 minutes with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Sauté garlic and kale, stir in white beans, warm through. Serve cod over the bean and greens mixture with a squeeze of lemon.

## Cottage Cheese and Berry Protein Bowl

A simple, high-protein snack or light meal that takes less than five minutes to put together on a day with no time to cook.

**Ingredients:** 1 cup cottage cheese, 1/2 cup mixed berries, 1 tbsp honey, 1 tbsp chopped nuts.

**Instructions:** Combine cottage cheese and berries in a bowl, drizzle with honey, top with chopped nuts.

## Turkey and Black Bean Stuffed Sweet Potatoes

A filling, protein-and-fiber-dense dinner that reheats well, making it a strong option for batch cooking ahead of a busy week.

**Ingredients:** 4 medium sweet potatoes, 1 lb ground turkey, 1 can black beans, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp chili powder, shredded cheese optional.

**Instructions:** Bake sweet potatoes at 400°F for 45 minutes until tender. Brown turkey with cumin and chili powder, stir in black beans. Split sweet potatoes, top with the turkey and bean mixture.

## Making these work for your kitchen

Ground chicken or a plant-based crumble works in place of turkey for the stuffed sweet potatoes, and any white fish substitutes for cod in the baked dinner. Greek yogurt swaps in for cottage cheese in the berry bowl if that's the texture you prefer.

## Batch cooking these for the week ahead

The egg muffins, quinoa bowl components, and stuffed sweet potatoes all reheat well, making a single larger cooking session on a weekend cover several days of higher-protein meals without cooking daily. Keeping cottage cheese and berries on hand separately covers the one recipe here that's better made fresh each time rather than prepped in advance.

## Why these recipes work together

Each one hits a meaningful protein number without relying on the same two or three go-to meals repeated all week, which matters for actually sustaining a higher protein target over months rather than a few motivated days. Variety here isn't just about boredom, it's what makes a higher protein target realistic long-term.
