Timing skincare adjustments to ovulation, tracking cycle length, adding food changes, is genuinely useful and also more than a lot of weeks have room for. If the full approach feels like too much, here's the single adjustment that captures most of the benefit.

## The one product worth having on hand

A salicylic acid spot treatment, used only on the couple of days you notice extra oil or the start of a breakout, addresses the core issue without requiring a full routine change. Keep it in your medicine cabinet and reach for it only when needed, rather than incorporating it into a daily routine you have to remember.

## The one timing habit worth building

Roughly note when your period starts each month, which you're likely already doing anyway, and expect oiliness to show up about two weeks later. This rough estimate is precise enough for this simplified version without needing detailed ovulation tracking.

## What to do if a breakout shows up despite this

Resist the urge to overcompensate with a much stronger product or more frequent washing once a breakout appears, since this often makes things worse rather than better. The spot treatment plus your normal gentle routine is enough, even once a breakout has started.

## What to skip for now

Detailed cycle tracking, food adjustments, and preventive skincare routines can all wait. The spot treatment and rough timing awareness cover the core idea without asking for more effort than you have available right now.

## What if breakouts feel worse than just "a little oil"

If what shows up is more than an occasional pimple, deeper, more widespread, or leaving marks behind, this simplified version may not be enough on its own, and that's a reasonable signal to consider a dermatologist conversation rather than continuing to manage it with a spot treatment alone.

## What this simplified version can and can't do

This won't prevent a breakout from happening as effectively as a fully proactive approach would, but it does help manage one once it shows up without adding complexity to your routine. If breakouts are severe or frustrating enough to want more prevention, building out the timing and food pieces later makes sense once you have more bandwidth.
