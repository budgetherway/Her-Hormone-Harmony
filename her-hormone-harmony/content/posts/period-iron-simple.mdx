Learning which foods enhance or block iron absorption, timing meals around coffee and calcium, and planning iron-rich recipes is a lot to manage during a week you're already dealing with your period. Here's the version that captures the core benefit with almost no planning.

## The one food pairing worth remembering

When you eat something iron-rich, spinach, lentils, beans, add a squeeze of lemon or a side of citrus. That single pairing covers the most important absorption-boosting step without needing to learn a longer list of enhancers and inhibitors.

## The one timing habit worth building

Have your coffee or tea at a different time than your main meals during your period, even just shifting it by an hour. This single adjustment protects iron absorption without requiring you to give up caffeine entirely.

## The easiest iron-rich food to keep on hand

Canned lentils or beans require no cooking beyond rinsing and heating, making them one of the lowest-effort iron sources available on a day cooking feels like too much. Adding them to whatever you're already eating, a salad, a wrap, rice, covers real ground without a dedicated recipe.

## What to skip for now

Detailed absorption research, precise meal timing, and cast iron cookware can all wait. The lemon pairing and coffee timing habit cover the highest-impact pieces without requiring more effort than that.

## What if exhaustion feels severe

If fatigue and dizziness are significant enough to interfere with daily functioning, that's worth a doctor's visit and a ferritin test rather than relying on this simplified food approach alone. Severe symptoms deserve a direct answer, not just dietary adjustment.

## What if you don't eat red meat

Canned lentils, beans, and fortified cereals still provide meaningful iron, just paired with a vitamin C source as consistently as possible since non-heme iron needs that pairing more than heme sources do. This simplified approach works for a plant-based diet too, it just leans a bit more on the lemon-pairing habit specifically.

## What this simplified version can and can't do

This captures the most impactful pieces of iron-focused eating without asking for a system to maintain. If symptoms don't improve after a couple of months, building out more structure, or getting bloodwork done, makes sense once you have more bandwidth to take it further.
