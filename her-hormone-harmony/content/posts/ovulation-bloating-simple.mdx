Tracking fluid balance, adjusting fiber timing, and planning meals around a narrow hormonal window is a lot to manage for a symptom that only lasts a day or two. Here's the version that addresses it with almost no planning required.

## The one habit worth keeping consistent

Keep drinking water normally, even though bloating makes it feel like you should cut back. This single habit, just not restricting water when you're already retaining fluid, prevents the situation from getting worse without requiring anything additional.

## The one food swap worth making

Reach for a banana or a handful of potassium-rich food when you notice bloating starting, rather than reaching for something salty. This small swap addresses the fluid balance piece directly without needing a broader meal plan built around it.

## The one movement habit worth adding

A ten-minute walk when bloating hits supports digestion mechanically and doesn't require any equipment, planning, or a dedicated workout session. This is genuinely one of the lowest-effort, most directly helpful things on this entire list.

## What to skip for now

Detailed cycle tracking, precise fiber timing, and planning meals in advance around this window can all wait. The three habits above cover the most helpful pieces without requiring you to anticipate anything in advance.

## What to wear when it hits

Choosing looser clothing on the day or two bloating shows up isn't a fix for the underlying cause, but it removes an easily avoidable source of added physical discomfort while the hormonal pattern runs its brief course.

## What this simplified version can and can't do

Since this bloating typically resolves within a day or two regardless of intervention, this simplified version is often enough to make those couple of days more comfortable without needing a fully mapped-out preventive plan. If bloating feels severe enough to want more prevention, building out the timing piece later, once you have bandwidth, can help catch it before it starts rather than just easing it once it has.
